# Agency Site

The marketing site of a B2B lead-generation agency. It showcases results and converts visitors into applicants.

## Language

**Agency**:
The B2B lead-generation business the site represents.
_Avoid_: Company, brand, firm

**Prospect**:
A visitor considering working with the Agency.
_Avoid_: Lead, client, customer, user

**Application**:
A Prospect's request to work with the Agency, made of name, company, and email.
_Avoid_: Quote, quote request, inquiry, contact form, sign-up

**Case Study**:
A long-form account of results the Agency produced for one client, shown with sample data.
_Avoid_: Success story, portfolio item, result

**Post**:
An educational blog article published by the Agency.
_Avoid_: Blog, article, resource

**Testimonial**:
A short attributed quote from a past client praising the Agency.
_Avoid_: Review, feedback
