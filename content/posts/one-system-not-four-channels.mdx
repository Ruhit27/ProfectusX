Most B2B teams run their channels in silos. One person sends cold email, another posts on LinkedIn, and an agency somewhere runs ads. Each channel gets measured on its own, and none of them knows what the others are doing.

## Connect the touchpoints

Picture a single prospect's experience. They see a useful post from your founder, get an email a week later that refers to the same problem, and then see a short case-study ad. Each touch makes the next one more credible.

## A simple wiring diagram

- **Content** builds familiarity and creates an engaged audience you can target.
- **Outbound** goes to the people engaging with content first, with messages that build on what they've read.
- **Retargeting** keeps proof such as case studies and results in front of anyone who visited the site.
- **Sales** hears about every touch, so the first call starts warm.

## Measure the system

Watch total qualified pipeline and cost per qualified meeting across all channels. Judging each channel alone undervalues the ones that warm prospects up for the others.
