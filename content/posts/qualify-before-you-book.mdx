Booking as many calls as possible feels like progress. But every unqualified meeting takes an hour of a rep's time and teaches the team to distrust outbound.

## Qualify in the thread

When a prospect replies with interest, ask one short question before sending a calendar link. Choose the question that best predicts a good deal for your business, for example:

- "Roughly how many people on your team would use this?"
- "Is this something you're looking to solve this quarter?"
- "Are you the right person to evaluate this, or should we include someone else?"

## Why one question works

One question doesn't slow down a motivated buyer, and it quietly filters out people who are only being polite. It also gives your rep context before the call starts.

## Track it

Tag each booked meeting with the answer, and review show rates and close rates by answer every month. Within a quarter you'll know exactly which answers lead to revenue.
