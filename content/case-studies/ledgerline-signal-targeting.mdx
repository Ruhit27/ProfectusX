> Sample case study for a fictional agency. The client and figures are invented.

## The challenge

Ledgerline automates month-end close for mid-market finance teams. Their outbound was hitting every CFO in a large database. Volume was high, relevance was low, and their domains were starting to suffer.

## What we did

- **Defined three buying signals:** a new controller or VP Finance hire, a recent funding round, and job posts mentioning a manual close process.
- **Monitored those signals weekly** and only added companies to campaigns when one fired.
- **Wrote messaging specific to each signal,** so a new controller heard about first-90-days wins and a funded company heard about scaling the finance team without adding headcount.

## The result

Ledgerline sent far fewer emails and booked twice as many meetings per send. Sales also said the conversations were better, because prospects were contacted at the moment the problem was most pressing.
