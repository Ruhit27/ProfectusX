> Sample case study for a fictional agency. The client and figures are invented.

## The challenge

Fieldnote sells a repository for user-research teams. Their two SDRs were sending from the primary domain, using a purchased list that mixed designers, marketers and researchers. Open rates had sunk and replies were mostly unsubscribes.

## What we did

- **Moved sending off the primary domain.** We set up six secondary domains with authenticated inboxes and warmed them for two weeks before any campaign went out.
- **Narrowed the list to one buyer.** We only targeted heads of research ops at companies that had posted a researcher role in the last 60 days.
- **Rewrote the sequence around one pain point.** Research gets lost in slide decks. Every email made that one point, in under 80 words.
- **Handled replies for the SDRs.** Interested replies went to a shared calendar link. Objections got a tested follow-up.

## The result

Within a month, demos per week had more than tripled. By day 90, outbound was Fieldnote's largest source of qualified pipeline, and the SDRs had moved from list-building to running demos.
