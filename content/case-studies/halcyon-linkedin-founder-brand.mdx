> Sample case study for a fictional agency. The client and figures are invented.

## The challenge

Halcyon's founder knew carrier operations better than almost anyone, but nobody outside their customer base had heard of them. Outbound worked, but every call started from zero trust.

## What we did

- **Interviewed the founder every two weeks** to collect stories, opinions and hard-won lessons.
- **Turned each interview into a content batch:** short posts, a carousel, and one longer breakdown of a real operational problem.
- **Ran engagement daily,** commenting on posts by carriers, brokers and shippers the team wanted to reach.
- **Linked content to outbound,** so prospects who engaged with a post got a warm, specific follow-up.

## The result

Within a quarter, prospects were mentioning the founder's posts on calls. Inbound demo requests appeared for the first time, and outbound reply rates rose because recipients already recognised the name.
